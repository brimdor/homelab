Production Prometheus does not enable its admin snapshot API. Do not call a disabled administrative endpoint, restart it merely to enable one, assume block-only copying preserves the current WAL/head, or silently accept missing recent samples. Prometheus storage guidance distinguishes a snapshot from copying only immutable blocks. A consistent protected capture and exercised restoration are software prerequisites. They are not evidence of a networking or hardware blocker. Real monitoring data must remain in approved protected remote storage; the owner's NAS write prohibition still applies.
