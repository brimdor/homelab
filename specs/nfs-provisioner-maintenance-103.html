Disposition

The earlier statement that security maintenance was unproven now has a narrower basis: official maintainers and a security-triage process are verified, while released-image patch/backport support remains unestablished by these sources. Do not describe the project as abandoned, claim an image is vulnerability-free, invent a newer application release, or infer patch support merely from a current repository.

NFS_PROVISIONER_UPDATE remains open. Its existing chart/image pair is current in the observed official index. NAS/export provenance, directory integrity and protected restore acceptance still require the existing trusted access and owner-authorized data workflow. The standing NAS prohibition remains binding. An image replacement or controller rollout would also require the exact identity, data safety, recovery and repository-first gates in system/nfs-client-provisioner/README.md. No synthetic PVC, mounted file, running controller or NAS endpoint was touched by this assessment.
