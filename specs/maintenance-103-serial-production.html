First production stages

metal/scripts/maintenance_production_node.py binds the first node to Totodile only. It streams configuration, boot files, worker identity and the K3s executable over verified SSH into an exclusive private recovery archive on Pikachu. No real backup is written to the workstation. Source and receiver success, byte count and full receiver SHA-256 readback are required. It preserves the existing recovery receiver and its other recovery points.

The controller composes the existing global ownership, native automatic-service hold, durable hop journal, exact-UID scheduling and serial eviction modules. The SSH child requests fresh Kubernetes evidence through the workstation's protected stdio connection; Kubernetes credentials remain on the workstation. Global ownership and the native hold persist on controller failure. PDB refusal or an uncertain eviction remains pending and is never replayed. Current Ceph health, active-node conditions and controller readiness gate each eviction and the source transaction. Only the target's node-pinned Rook crash/exporter observers may wait for its return. The initial controller covers backup, holds, cordon, drain and source-package preparation; it does not mark later upgrade/reboot/release stages or any parent maintenance task complete.

Production dispatch now also records target download and offline-reboot intent.
