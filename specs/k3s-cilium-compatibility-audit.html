Transition rules and rejected shortcuts

  • Cilium's upgrade guide requires adjacent minor transitions and the latest patch in the current minor first. A future candidate needs every intermediate release's notes, exact immutable artifacts, CRD/value compatibility and preflight results. Do not use a multi-minor leap or --reuse-values to bridge the gap.
  • Keep the initial-version upgradeCompatibility contract and all existing values under review. L7 proxy traffic may disconnect during agent upgrades. DNS, policy allow/deny behavior, API access without kube-proxy, L2 service reachability, Hubble and application connections require separate acceptance after each step.
  • K3s upgrades require consecutive Kubernetes minors, one server at a time before agents, and preserved installation settings. Restarting the installer does not itself drain workloads or preserve omitted flags.
  • Do not downgrade production Kubernetes to fit an old Cilium row. That would require its own supported datastore and binary recovery design; the matrix is not downgrade authorization.
  • Do not combine a CNI transition with Fedora/kernel, GPU, Ceph/CSI or control-plane changes. The Fedora 44 audit and conditional node runbook retain their unresolved package, device and recovery gates.
