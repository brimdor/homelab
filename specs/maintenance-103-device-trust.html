How trust is established

Obtain public material from the device console, a previously verified administrator session, or an existing protected inventory with known provenance. Bind the hostname/IP, device identity, protocol/port, algorithm and observation time. A fingerprint first fetched over the same untrusted connection is not independent verification. Preserve normal certificate hostname, chain and expiry checks; never disable them. Public certificates and host keys suffice; private keys, passwords and tokens are not needed in chat.
