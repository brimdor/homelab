Kured compatibility correction

Objective and evidence

CHART_MINOR_UPDATES in canonical maintenance issue 103 includes supported Kured maintenance. On 2026-10-19, chart 5.12.0 runs Kured 1.22.0 on Kubernetes 1.33. Official 1.22.0 notes list Kubernetes 1.34–1.36; 1.21.0 notes list 1.33–1.35. Correct only runtime image to 1.21.0 at sha256:76567a71427b074bfc51df6797c8c30ba44c9678ace7cd61a186ddb662ce97b1.
