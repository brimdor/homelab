Failure handling and rollback

Before failover, the original application remains the authority. A failed or changed replica does not justify deleting its claim or changing the source. Never use forced promotion, force detach, a stale RDB overwrite or a timeout increase to manufacture success.

After handoff, the new master is the write authority. A Git revert alone must not restart an empty old sidecar as authority. Keep the new master and claim, restore application routing to that master through Git, and diagnose any failed application startup. Reverse replication and another coordinated handoff require their own verified current identities and durable intent. Do not erase either store on uncertainty.

Primary contracts and limits

Valkey's coordinated FAILOVER pauses source writes, waits for the chosen replica, demotes the source and requests promotion. It is asynchronous; FORCE and unsafe ABORT are excluded. Its replication contract distinguishes synchronization history from general strong consistency. These mechanisms do not establish whole-homelab recovery or NAS provenance. No new human approval is requested by this design; existing explicit gates and NAS restrictions still apply.
