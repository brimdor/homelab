The QEMU manual documents socket backends and hub ports. The QEMU socket implementation frames Ethernet with a network-order32-bit length and restores its listener after a client disconnects. Bind every listener explicitly to127.0.0.1. Local listeners are not an authentication boundary against other local processes.
